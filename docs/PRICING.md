# Pricing strategy

FashionFunks is positioned as an accessible mid-market fashion concept for India rather than a luxury storefront.

## Market benchmark used for V3

Public India storefront checks in August 2026 were used as a sanity check rather than converting US-dollar sample prices:

- H&M India men's basic T-shirts commonly sit around ₹499–₹1,499.
- H&M India casual dresses commonly sit around ₹999–₹3,999, with many everyday options in the ₹1,499–₹2,799 range.
- H&M India women's shoes commonly sit around ₹999–₹2,499, with some premium styles higher.
- Zara India men's T-shirts commonly sit around ₹950–₹2,950.
- Zara India men's shirts commonly sit around ₹2,950–₹4,350.

FashionFunks intentionally targets the lower-to-middle portion of those ranges for most essentials, while allowing a smaller number of statement jackets and limited-edition pieces to sit above the everyday range.

## Catalogue guardrails

| Product type | Typical FashionFunks band |
| --- | ---: |
| Kids basics | ₹799–₹1,599 |
| Everyday T-shirts | ₹1,099–₹1,499 |
| Shirts / tops | ₹1,599–₹2,299 |
| Trousers | ₹1,999–₹2,599 |
| Dresses | ₹1,499–₹3,299 |
| Sweatshirts / hoodies | ₹1,799–₹2,499 |
| Everyday outerwear | ₹2,499–₹3,999 |
| Limited graphic / fandom pieces | ₹1,499–₹4,299 |

The catalogue is fictional and does not claim to reproduce another retailer's exact pricing. These bands exist to keep the portfolio experience commercially believable.

## Order economics used in the demo

- Free delivery from ₹1,999.
- Standard delivery below the threshold: ₹199.
- Demo basket promotion: 5% off orders above ₹5,000.

No real payment is processed and no real order is fulfilled.
